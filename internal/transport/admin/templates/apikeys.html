How the keys protect your apps

Publishable key ({{.PKPrefix}}…) goes into the web or mobile app and is sent as X-Warden-Key. It tells Warden which app is calling. Bound to origins, a browser on any other site gets origin_not_allowed, and its origins are added to CORS automatically. It is not a password: anything shipped to users can be read, so user data stays protected by access tokens, PKCE and refresh token rotation.

Secret key ({{.SKPrefix}}…) stays on your servers and is sent as Authorization: Bearer to {{.PublicURL}}/v1/server/…. Warden stores only its SHA-256 hash, rejects it on requests that carry a browser Origin, and rate limits it per key.
